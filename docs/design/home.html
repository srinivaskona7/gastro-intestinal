High-Yield USMLE GI Pearls

[PEARL 01] • BIOCHEM & HEPATOLOGY verified

SAAG Calculation & Transudative vs Exudative

SAAG ≥ 1.1 g/dL indicates portal hypertension (cirrhosis, congestive heart failure, Budd-Chiari syndrome). SAAG < 1.1 g/dL indicates peritoneal carcinomatosis, peritoneal tuberculosis, or nephrotic syndrome.

[PEARL 02] • VASCULAR ANATOMY verified

Watershed Ischemia & Griffith's Point

Splenic flexure (SMA / IMA junction) and rectosigmoid junction (IMA / superior rectal) are most vulnerable to hypotensive shock and non-occlusive mesenteric ischemia; present with postprandial cramping and bloody diarrhea.

[PEARL 03] • IMMUNOLOGY & HISTOLOGY verified

Coeliac Disease Serology & Biopsy Triad

Screen with anti-tissue transglutaminase (tTG-IgA) paired with total IgA to rule out deficiency. Confirmation via duodenal biopsy demonstrating intraepithelial lymphocytosis, crypt hyperplasia, and villous blunting.

[PEARL 04] • ENDOCRINE NEOPLASMS verified

VIPoma Classic Triad (WDHA Syndrome)

Characterized by Watery Diarrhea, Hypokalemia, and Achlorhydria (WDHA syndrome). Non-beta islet cell pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor diagnosed via elevated fasting vasoactive intestinal peptide.
