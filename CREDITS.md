## 3D model (`/atlas-3d/`)

`public/models/gi-tract.glb` is derived from **BodyParts3D**, &copy; The Database Center for Life Science,
licensed under **CC Attribution 4.0 International** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Licence verified on the database's own page, https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html
(last updated 2025-02-27, read 2026-10-03). Dataset: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html.
Citation: Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), doi:10.1093/nar/gkn613.

Older BodyParts3D releases and OBJ file comments say CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan; the current database licence
above is CC BY 4.0 and permits redistribution and adaptation. The meshes were taken from the
[ashemag/human-atlas](https://github.com/ashemag/human-atlas) pack, which states it is built from the
BodyParts3D 4.0 archive (`isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip`) and already simplified it. That repository's statement
was not independently matched to the 4.0 archive here.

Adaptations: 13 organs extracted (the liver is assembled from the caudate lobe and hepatic segments II to IX),
merged per organ into one GLB, normals recomputed in the viewer, recoloured. Rebuild with
`python3 scripts/convert-bp3d.py <dir>` (see the script header). Rendering uses three.js (MIT).
