## What it is

**Cystic fibrosis (CF)** is an inherited disease of **exocrine glands**. Their secretions become abnormally thick and salty, so ducts block and organs are slowly damaged. The lungs, pancreas, intestines and skin are affected most.

## Cause

CF is a **simple autosomal recessive** condition caused by mutation of the **CFTR gene on chromosome 7**. A child needs two faulty copies. Roughly **1 in 25 people are carriers**.

## Pathogenesis

CFTR codes for an **active chloride channel**, which normally helps regulate the salt content of secretions. When it fails, the secretions turn thick and salty. They then obstruct the narrow ducts of the organs that make them.

## Clinical features

| Organ | What happens |
|---|---|
| Lungs | Thicker mucus and poor clearance cause frequent infections. Crepitations (crackles) and rhonchi (wheezy, rattling sounds) are heard on the stethoscope |
| Pancreas | A blocked pancreatic duct causes subclinical pancreatitis, which progresses to chronic pancreatic failure |
| Intestines | Poor digestion and malabsorption lead to malnutrition |
| Reproductive ducts | Obstructed vas deferens causes infertility |
| Sweat glands | Salty sweat; hyponatraemia (low sodium) if salt is not replaced |

## Investigations

- **Spirometry** shows an **obstructive pattern** with reduced FEV1.
- **Chest X-ray** shows **gas trapping and hyperinflation**.
- **Genetic testing** is the **definitive** test.

## Management

| Problem | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Pancreatic insufficiency | Enzyme replacement (for example Creon Forte) |
| Salt loss | Salt tablets |
| Fat-soluble vitamin deficiency | Vitamins A, D, E and K (ADEK) |
| Sputum retention | Chest physiotherapy: percussion and postural drainage |
| Viscous mucus | Mucolytics such as DNase, which breaks down extracellular DNA and lowers mucus viscosity |
| Recurrent infection | Antibiotics, for example tobramycin |

## Prognosis

Median life expectancy is about **40 years**.

<Callout kind="pearl">
Fat malabsorption is why ADEK vitamins are replaced: A, D, E and K are fat-soluble and are lost with undigested fat.
</Callout>
