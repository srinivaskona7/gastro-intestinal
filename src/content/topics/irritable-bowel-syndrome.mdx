## What it is

**Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)**, sometimes called "spastic colon", is a **functional disorder**: the bowel behaves abnormally but looks normal. It is an umbrella term and a **diagnosis of exclusion**, meaning it is made only after other causes have been ruled out.

<Anatomy figure="lower-gi" highlight={['transverse-colon', 'descending-colon', 'sigmoid-colon']} caption="Lower gi: transverse colon, descending colon, sigmoid colon" />

## Causes and triggers

The cause is unknown. Recognised triggers and contributors include:

- Stress, anxiety and depression
- Pain
- Autonomic irregularities
- Gut hypersensitivity

## Pathogenesis and morphology

Because it is functional, there is likely **no pathology**. Biopsy and gross inspection are normal. The disorder is thought to arise from the gut-brain interaction (mood, autonomic control and a hypersensitive gut) rather than from damaged tissue.

## Clinical features

- Abdominal pain, discomfort or bloating that is **related to defecation**
- **Chronic** course, lasting more than 3 months
- **Alternating bowel habit**: diarrhoea or constipation, with or without tenesmus (feeling of incomplete evacuation)

<Callout kind="warning" title="Red flags exclude IBS">
Acute onset, anaemia, fever, blood in the stool or weight loss suggest other pathology. Investigate rather than label as IBS.
</Callout>

## Investigations

Tests in IBS are normal; they are done to exclude alternatives.

| Test | Looking for |
|---|---|
| Stool ova, cysts and parasites | Parasitic infection |
| Stool culture | Bacterial infection or small intestinal bacterial overgrowth |
| Allergy or intolerance testing | Lactose intolerance |
| Duodenal biopsy | Coeliac disease |
| Colonoscopy if over 50 | Bowel cancer |

## Management

- **Diet**: avoid foods the patient is intolerant of, and add fibre.
- **Anti-diarrhoeals** such as loperamide.
- **Probiotics**, as yoghurts or tablets.

<Callout kind="pearl">
Normal tests plus chronic pain linked to defecation and altered habit, with no red flags, is IBS. A new change in bowel habit after 50 needs colonoscopy first.
</Callout>
