## What it is

A **carcinoid tumour** is a **neuroendocrine tumour** of the intestines whose cells make **serotonin** (5-HT). A primary bowel tumour often causes no symptoms at all. **Carcinoid syndrome** is the set of symptoms produced when the hormones reach the general circulation, which in practice means the tumour has **metastasised**, usually to the liver.

<Anatomy figure="lower-gi" highlight={['appendix', 'ileum']} caption="Lower gi: appendix, ileum" />

## Where it arises

Three sites account for most cases:

- **Appendix**
- **Terminal ileum**
- **Rectum**

## Pathogenesis

The tumour releases several active substances: **serotonin, bradykinin, histamine and prostaglandins**. Together they cause **diarrhoea** and **cardiac complications**.

Hormones made by a gut tumour normally drain through the portal vein to the liver, where they are broken down. Liver metastases release them straight into the hepatic veins and so into the systemic circulation, which is why the syndrome only appears once the tumour has spread.

## Clinical features

| Feature | Detail |
|---|---|
| Before spread | Usually asymptomatic |
| Skin | Hot flushes |
| Gut | Chronic watery diarrhoea and abdominal pain |
| Heart | Right-sided abnormalities: pulmonary stenosis or tricuspid regurgitation |
| Liver | Hepatomegaly (from metastases) |

<Callout kind="pearl">
Carcinoid heart disease is **right-sided** because the lungs inactivate the hormones before blood returns to the left heart. Think tricuspid regurgitation and pulmonary stenosis.
</Callout>

## Investigations

- **CT** to find the tumour and spread.
- **Ultrasound** to look for liver metastases.
- **Echocardiography** to detect carcinoid heart disease (valvular disease).
- **Urine 5-HIAA**, the breakdown product of serotonin, which is raised.

## Management

- **Somatostatin analogues** treat symptoms, reducing flushing and diarrhoea.
- **Tumour resection**, with or without **cardiac valvuloplasty or repair** when the valves are damaged.

## Complications

The main complication is **metastasis**, which is also what triggers the syndrome. Cardiac valve damage is the other major consequence.
