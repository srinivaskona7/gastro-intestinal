## What it is

Gallbladder carcinoma is a malignant tumour of the gallbladder wall. Under the microscope it shows the features of an **adenocarcinoma**.

## Causes and pathogenesis

The main cause is **chronic cholelithiasis**. Gallstones keep the gallbladder wall irritated and inflamed for years, and that persistent inflammation can eventually give rise to a neoplasm.

## Clinical features

- It is **more common in females**, matching the sex distribution of gallstones.
- Symptoms are non-specific: **vague abdominal pain**, **anorexia** and **weight loss**.

Because nothing points clearly to the gallbladder, the tumour is usually discovered late.

## Investigations

| Test | Role |
|---|---|
| LFTs | Raised ALP and GGT |
| CT | Diagnosis and staging |

## Management

| Situation | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Below stage 3 | Surgical resection |
| Metastatic disease | Palliative chemotherapy |

## Prognosis

Late diagnosis is typical, so the prognosis is **poor**.

<Callout kind="pearl">
Gallstones for years, plus a woman with vague abdominal pain and weight loss, should make you think of gallbladder carcinoma as well as simple biliary colic.
</Callout>
