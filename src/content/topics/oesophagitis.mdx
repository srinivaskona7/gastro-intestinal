## What it is and what causes it

**Oesophagitis** is inflammation of the oesophageal lining. The cause decides the appearance and the treatment.

| Cause | Detail |
|---|---|
| Infection | In immunocompromised patients: **Candida**, **herpes**, **CMV** |
| Allergic (eosinophilic) | Food allergy |
| GORD | Gastro-oesophageal reflux disease, acid washing up into the oesophagus |

<Anatomy figure="oesophagus" highlight={['oesophagus', 'lower-oesophageal-sphincter']} caption="Oesophagus: oesophagus, lower oesophageal sphincter" />

## Appearance at endoscopy

| Type | Morphology |
|---|---|
| Candida | Characteristic **white plaques** |
| Eosinophilic | **Mucosal furrowing**, thickened mucosa, plaques of surface exudate |

## Clinical features

- Dysphagia
- Heartburn
- Oesophageal pain

These overlap heavily across causes, so the history alone does not identify the type.

## Investigation

- **Endoscopy with biopsy** and microscopy, culture and sensitivity (MCS).
- **Full blood count with differential**: eosinophilia suggests an allergic cause.
- **HIV screen**, because infective oesophagitis points to immunocompromise.

## Management

  caption="From cause to treatment"
  chart={`flowchart TD

- **Candida:** oral fluconazole.
- **Allergic:** inhaled fluticasone or budesonide (swallowed rather than breathed in, so the steroid coats the oesophagus).

<Callout kind="pearl">
Candida oesophagitis in a patient with no obvious reason for immune weakness should prompt an HIV test.
</Callout>
