## What they are

Two unrelated conditions produce a space-occupying lesion in the liver.

| | Pyogenic abscess | Hydatid cyst |
|---|---|---|
| Cause | Bacterial infection | Echinococcus granulosus parasite |
| Number | Single or multiple | Single or multiple |
| Nature | Collection of pus | Parasitic cyst |

## Liver abscess

### Causes

Infection, typically by **E. coli** or other **anaerobes**, reaches the liver from:

- **Intra-abdominal sepsis** (infection spreading from the bowel or abdomen)
- **Biliary sepsis**, particularly in the **elderly**

### Pathogenesis and morphology

Bacteria seed the liver and a **pyogenic abscess** (a pus-filled cavity) forms.

### Clinical features

The picture ranges from **no symptoms to acute illness**.

- Symptoms: malaise, fever and rigors, anorexia, weight loss, vomiting, abdominal pain
- Signs: the patient may be septic, jaundiced and febrile, and the liver is **tender and enlarged**

### Investigations

| Test | Finding |
|---|---|
| Alkaline phosphatase | Raised |
| Bilirubin | Raised |
| Blood cultures | Positive |
| Abdominal ultrasound | Definitive |
| Abdominal CT | Helps rule out hepatocellular carcinoma |

### Treatment and prognosis

- **Ultrasound-guided aspiration** of the pus
- **Antibiotics**: metronidazole plus gentamicin

A **single** abscess has a good prognosis. **Multiple** abscesses carry about **80 percent mortality**.

## Hydatid disease of the liver

### Cause

Infection by the parasite **Echinococcus granulosus**, which is spread by **dogs and sheep**.

### Pathogenesis

One or more **cysts** form, typically in the **lower right lobe** of the liver.

### Morphology

Each cyst has **three layers**:

1. **Outer**: derived from the host
2. **Intermediate**: a laminated layer
3. **Inner**: the germinal layer, which buds off **brood capsules** that become **daughter cysts**

<Mermaid
  caption="Layers of a hydatid cyst, outside to inside"
  chart={`flowchart TD
  A["Outer layer from host"] --> B["Intermediate laminated layer"]
  B --> C["Inner germinal layer"]
  C --> D["Brood capsules"]
  D --> E["Daughter cysts"]`}
/>

### Clinical features

Often **asymptomatic**. Some patients have a **dull ache in the right upper quadrant**.

### Treatment

- **Albendazole**, an antiparasitic drug
- **Surgical aspiration**, by fine needle or open surgery
